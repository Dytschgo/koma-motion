# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Koma Motion is an open-source project. We want everyone who takes part in it to
have a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate in every interaction.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Show empathy towards other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information of other people without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other channel used for the project. It also
applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub:

1. Use the **Report content** option that GitHub offers on the comment, issue
   or pull request in question.
2. If you need to reach the maintainers directly, contact the repository owner
   through their GitHub profile.

All reports are reviewed and handled with discretion. Maintainers who receive a
report will keep the identity of the reporter confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
